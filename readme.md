This is Andrew Ellis's final lab: Chess with White Pieces! You should be able to run the program by downloading the tar file, then running "make run" in the terminal. Or, you can just run ./main.
After running the program, you should see helpful lines that demonstrate how to utilize the program. To exit or quit the program, just enter 'q'. To see a list of the commands again, enter 'c'. To move a piece, enter 'm'. Afterward, you'll be prompted to enter the cell of the piece you want to move. Finally, after entering that, you'll enter the cell that you want your piece to be moved to. For example, if you want to move your pawn on e2 to e4, you simply enter "e2", and then "e4" when prompted again.
Disclaimer: This program requires users to know the rules of chess, and is not a full implementation of chess. With that being said, the following rules are not implemented:
En Pessant, Castling, Not Capturing Your Own Pieces, Moving Across Opposite Sides of the Board, Check, and Checkmate.
